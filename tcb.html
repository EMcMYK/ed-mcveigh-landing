A Professional Online Presence for TCB Commercial Cleaning Service

When Bill, the owner of TCB Commercial Cleaning Service, approached me for a new website, his goal was clear: he wanted a clean, professional, and easy-to-navigate online presence that would serve as an additional avenue for potential clients to find and contact his business.

To meet his needs, I designed a sleek, single-page website that focuses on simplicity and functionality. The layout ensures that all essential information—such as services, client testimonials, and contact details—is easily accessible without overwhelming visitors. The design reflects the professionalism and reliability of TCB's services, with a crisp, modern aesthetic that aligns perfectly with the cleaning industry.

In addition to the website, I refreshed the TCB logo to give the brand a more contemporary and cohesive look. The updated logo complements the website design, reinforcing the company's identity and making it more memorable to potential clients.

This project was all about creating an impactful yet straightforward online presence, giving TCB Commercial Cleaning Service a professional edge in today's competitive market. With its responsive design, the website ensures that visitors have a seamless experience whether they're browsing on a desktop, tablet, or smartphone.
